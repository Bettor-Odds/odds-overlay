Odds Overlay

Turns the percentages in Novig back into American odds — right on top of the app.

Download for Android

Free. Nothing leaves your phone. Not on the Play Store — installs directly.

Heads up: because this draws over other apps, Google won't list it in the Play Store, so your phone will ask a couple of times if you're sure. You are. Just tap allow.

After it downloads

1

Open the file

Tap the download. If it says the file can't be opened, tap Settings then turn on Allow from this source and go back.

2

Open Odds Overlay and tap Start

It'll ask for three things. Tap Allow on notifications, then Allow on "Display over other apps."

3

Tap "Start now" on the recording screen

Android warns it will "capture your screen." That's how it reads the odds — it stays on your phone and nothing is recorded or sent. Tap Start now.

4

Open Novig

The percentages are now American odds. Turn it off any time from the notification.
