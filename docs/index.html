American Odds Converter

by Bettor Odds

Turns the percentages in prediction market apps back into American odds — drawn right on top of the app, live as you scroll.

Download for Android

Free. Nothing leaves your phone. Not on the Play Store — installs directly.

Heads up: to read the odds on screen it uses Android's accessibility setting, so the permission screen mentions "view and control your screen." It only reads the odds - nothing is recorded, nothing leaves your phone. Not in the Play Store; installs directly.

After it downloads

1

Open the file to install

Tap the download. If it warns you, tap Settings then turn on Allow from this source and go back.

2

Open Odds Converter and tap Enable

It sends you to Accessibility settings. Find Odds Converter, turn it on, and confirm. On Android 13+, if it's greyed out, open the app info menu first and tap Allow restricted settings.

3

That's it - open your app

A "Show American odds?" prompt pops up. Tap Turn on and the percentages become American odds. Pick Always to skip the prompt next time. It switches off on its own when you leave the app.
