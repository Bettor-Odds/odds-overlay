American Odds Converter

by Bettor Odds

Turns the percentages in prediction market apps back into American odds — drawn right on top of the app, live as you scroll.

Download for Android

Free. Reads odds on-device; only goes online to check for updates. Not on the Play Store — installs directly.

Heads up: to read the odds on screen it uses Android's accessibility setting, so the permission screen mentions "view and control your screen." It only reads the odds - nothing is recorded. Not in the Play Store; installs directly.

Set up (about 2 minutes, once)

1

Install it

Tap the downloaded file. If it warns you, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back, and install.

2

Allow restricted settings

Because it installs outside the Play Store, Android hides the switch until you unlock it:

Settings › Apps › Odds Converter › ⋮ (top-right) › Allow restricted settings

Can't find the app? Tap See all apps first. No "restricted settings" line? Your phone doesn't need this - skip to step 3.

3

Turn on the service

Settings › Accessibility › Odds Converter › turn it On › Allow

It says "view and control your screen" - that's how it reads the odds. It only reads them; nothing is recorded and nothing leaves your phone.

4

Open a prediction market app

A "Show American odds?" prompt pops up - tap Turn on. The percentages become American odds, live as you scroll. Tap Always to skip the prompt next time. It turns off on its own when you leave the app.
